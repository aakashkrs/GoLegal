Advertisement Details: Consultants in ED Division

Post Consultant in ED Division of the Ministry of External Affairs, Headquarters, New Delhi
Period of Consultancy Initially 1 year, extendable based on performance and mutual agreement.
Nature of Duties - Monitoring and follow-up of legal issues
- Providing legal advice on arbitration cases
- Attending court hearings and preparing legal briefs
- Advising on UNCITRAL and International Solar Alliance matters
- Participating in treaty interpretation and negotiations
- Analyzing legal issues and recommending actions
- Any other duties assigned by Head of Division
Job Location Ministry of External Affairs, New Delhi
Qualifications/Essential Criteria - Indian national
- Post Graduate Degree in Law (LLM) with specialization in arbitration/commercial/investment matters
- Minimum 3 years post-LLM work experience
- Registered to practice before Indian courts
- Age between 26-45 years
Desirable Criteria - Previous experience in investment treaty arbitration or investment treaty law in the Government of India
- Strong written and spoken English skills
- Proficiency in computer applications
Remuneration & Entitlements - Maximum remuneration of Rs. 10 lakh per annum
- Strictly contractual, no additional benefits like PF, Pension, etc.
- No HRA admissible
- Full-time engagement with specified working hours
- Paid leave subject to conditions
- Travel expenses covered by Ministry if required
How to Apply - Submit application in prescribed proforma along with supporting documents
- Offline mode: By registered post to Ministry of External Affairs, New Delhi
- Online mode: Via email to aopfsec@mea.gov.in
- Shortlisted candidates will be notified for interview
- No TA/DA provided for interview
- Final selection based on interview performance
Application Deadline May 14, 2024, 17:30 hrs
