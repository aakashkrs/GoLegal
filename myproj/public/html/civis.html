Advertisement Details: Consultantio Fellowship in Civis

A Non-Profit Organization

About Civis offers the Consultatio Fellowship for new law graduates interested in public policy in India. Supported by Manupatra, it provides hands-on experience in various aspects of law-making and citizen engagement.
Fellowship Duration 1 year full-time in-person fellowship
Roles and Responsibilities - Facilitating and managing public consultations on draft laws and policies
- Ideating and promoting citizen participation in lawmaking
- Leading and managing volunteer teams
- Building partnerships with public officials and institutions
- Working on outreach and communications
- Assisting in platform functioning and feedback gathering
Eligibility - Law degree essential
- 0-2 years of policy analysis experience preferred
- Experience with law reviews/journals/magazines editing preferred
Required Skills - Ability to learn content management system
- Interest in civic engagement
- Preferred: Experience in mobilizing students for college clubs/societies
Stipend Rs. 45,000 PM
Location Mumbai

How to Fill Application Form Step by Step Process

  1. Click on the provided link for the application form.
  2. Enter your Email ID.
  3. Choose "Yes" to apply for the Consultatio Fellowship.
  4. Proceed to the next page.
  5. Enter your Full Name and Mobile number.
  6. Provide reasons for your interest in the role and why you are suitable for it.
  7. Rate your proficiency levels.
  8. Choose your relocation status to Mumbai.
  9. Upload your updated Resume.
  10. Click on the Submit button.

You’ll receive a confirmation of successful submission either on the screen or via email.
