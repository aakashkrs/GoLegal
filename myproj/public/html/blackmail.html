ABOUT CYBER CRIME REPORTING PORTAL

The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal is an initiative by the Government of India aimed
at facilitating citizens to report cybercrime incidents online. Launched in 2019 by the Ministry
of Home Affairs, the portal provides a platform for individuals to report various types of
cybercrimes such as financial frauds, online harassment, cyberbullying, identity theft, hacking,
and more.

The portal enables citizens to register complaints without the need to visit a police station
physically, making it more convenient and accessible. Upon registering a complaint, the portal
directs the information to the relevant law enforcement agencies for further investigation and
action.

The objective of the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal is to streamline the process of
reporting cybercrimes, enhance cybersecurity awareness among citizens, and facilitate quicker
responses from law enforcement agencies to tackle cyber threats effectively. This initiative is
part of the government's broader efforts to combat cybercrime and ensure a safer digital
environment for all citizens.


THE KEY POINTS OF THE NATIONAL CYBER CRIME REPORTING PORTAL:

1. Online Reporting: The portal provides a platform for citizens to report cybercrime
incidents online, eliminating the need to visit a police station physically.

2. Various Cybercrimes: Citizens can report a wide range of cybercrimes including
financial frauds, online harassment, cyberbullying, identity theft, hacking, phishing,
and more.

3. Convenience: The portal offers convenience by allowing citizens to report
cybercrimes from anywhere with an internet connection, at any time.

4. Anonymous Reporting: Individuals have the option to report cybercrimes
anonymously if they wish to protect their identity.

5. Efficient Handling: Reported complaints are directed to the relevant law
enforcement agencies for investigation and action, ensuring efficient handling of
cybercrime incidents.

6. Nationwide Coverage: The portal caters to citizens across India, providing a
nationwide platform for reporting cybercrimes.

7. Enhanced Cybersecurity Awareness: By encouraging citizens to report cybercrimes,
the portal contributes to enhancing cybersecurity awareness and vigilance among the
general public.

8. Government Initiative: The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal is an initiative
of the Government of India, launched under the Ministry of Home Affairs, to combat
cybercrimes effectively and ensure a safer digital environment for all citizens.

These key points highlight the significance of the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal in
facilitating the reporting and investigation of cybercrimes in India.

PROCESS OF AFTER FILING COMPLAINT


➢ Within 3 to 7 business days, your complaint will be forwarded to the local concerned
cyber police station.

➢ Every district has its own cyber police station.
➢ The complaint will be investigated and resolved by the concerned cyber police station

in your area.
➢ After that, an investigating officer will be assigned to you, who will investigate your

filed complaint.
➢ In most cases, the mobile number of the assigned investigating officer will be shared

with the complainant via SMS or email.
➢ If you don’t get any status on your filed complaint via online tracking system of

the concerned government department.

➢ You may also contact them through the provided number to inquire about the
status of your filed complaint.

➢ If you don’t receive any update on filed complaint, you may file an RTI for seeking
response.

HERE'S A STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE TO THE RTI APPLICATION PROCESS,
INCLUDING THE FIRST APPEAL, SECOND APPEAL, AND APPROACHING THE
HIGH COURT IF NEEDED:

1. RTI APPLICATION:
➢ Write down your query or request clearly and concisely on a plain paper or use

the prescribed format provided by the respective government department.
➢ Address the application to the designated Public Information Officer (PIO) of

the concerned department.
➢ Submit the application along with the required fee, if any, through online or

offline mode.


2. FIRST APPEAL:
➢ If you do not receive a response to your RTI application within the stipulated

time frame (generally 30 days), or if you are dissatisfied with the response
received, you can file a first appeal.

➢ File the first appeal within 30 days from the expiry of the prescribed time limit
or from the date of receipt of the decision, whichever is earlier.

➢ Address the appeal to the First Appellate Authority (FAA) of the concerned
department.

➢ The FAA must dispose of the appeal within 30 days of its receipt.

3. SECOND APPEAL:
➢ If you do not receive a satisfactory response from the FAA or if your appeal is

rejected, you can file a second appeal.
➢ File the second appeal within 90 days from the date on which the decision

should have been made by the FAA or from the date of receipt of the decision.
➢ Address the second appeal to the State Information Commission (SIC) or the

Central Information Commission (CIC), depending on the jurisdiction.
➢ The SIC or CIC will conduct a hearing and issue a decision within the stipulated

time frame.

4. APPROACHING HIGH COURT:
➢ If you are not satisfied with the decision of the SIC or CIC, or if the issue

remains unresolved even after the second appeal, you can approach the High
Court.

➢ File a writ petition under Article 226 of the Constitution of India in the High
Court having jurisdiction over the matter.

➢ The High Court will review the case and issue appropriate directions or orders
based on the merits of the case.

5. LIMITATION PERIODS:
➢ The limitation period for filing the first appeal is 30 days from the expiry of the

prescribed time limit or from the date of receipt of the decision.

➢ The limitation period for filing the second appeal is 90 days from the date on
which the decision should have been made by the FAA or from the date of
receipt of the decision.


IT'S IMPORTANT TO ADHERE TO THESE LIMITATION PERIODS TO ENSURE
TIMELY REDRESSAL OF GRIEVANCES UNDER THE RTI ACT.


NOTE-

➢ In case an investigation into your filed complaint has not been initiated or the status
of your complaint remains undisclosed, you have the option to approach the Hon’ble
Court under Section 156(3) of the Criminal Procedure Code (CrPC), along with a
complaint under Section 200 of the CrPC.

➢ Upon filing, the Court will schedule a date for hearing your case. Following
deliberation, the Court may choose to take cognizance of the matter.

➢ Subsequently, the Court will issue directives to the concerned officer at the Cyber
Police Station or the Police Station within the relevant district. The Court may also
request an action taken report, commonly known as a status report, on your filed
complaint.

This approach provides a legal recourse to ensure that your complaint is duly addressed
and investigated by the appropriate authorities.
